Ryan's final October 6 scope: **port the full current Easy Eject project to Windows**, not an Insta360-only or travel MVP. Baseline: `384cb767ffbbb87ec5fe67f45d0b4d0ce324ad7c` (website 1.7.8 preview), including Luna information and manual Quick Share. ## Staged delivery

1. **Insta360 workflow and physical proof.** Identify the model/reader/card/destination with Ryan; establish storage mode and media/package layout; validate detection, complete original copies, cancellation, reconnect and safe manual eject on explicitly authorized disposable hardware. Retain originals. Finish UI/status behavior on this laptop, including scaling, tray lifecycle and Windows veto explanations.
2. **Complete import parity.** Port media/package selection, companion preservation, preview options, clean/preserved layouts and conflict-group reuse. Add profile management/shared defaults, explicit custom overrides, migration and operation-frozen options. Include optional original deletion and Trash recovery with the Mac confirmations, re-verification and fail-closed policy, but never exercise deletion on real media overnight. Implement completion/eject choices, audit/history and useful issue notices. Automatic actions remain off unless deliberately enabled.
3. **Complete app/settings and manual sharing.** Port grouped card actions, optional cleanup, launch-at-login choice, SSD confirmation, notifications, shortcut, debug/issue settings and the full manual Quick Share workflow. Windows-native equivalents must preserve behavior. Luna Ultra/Pro MP4 and Luna I-Log support is narrow, not proof of arbitrary Insta360 conversion. Keep exports manual, verify saved originals, and reserve destination storage.
4. **Travel rehearsal and signed direct distribution.** Rehearse representative long/large captures, insufficient space, interrupts and restart on the travel setup. After hardware/runtime acceptance, prepare a versioned direct-download artifact, supported signing and a simple installation/update path. Public release requires tested evidence and publishing authorization. Do not call the port complete while applicable parity or physical proof is missing.

These stages describe order across the available month, not promised weekly completion dates. The current overnight heartbeat remains bounded to October 6 at 08:00 America/New_York; it does not schedule a month of unattended work.

## Manual Quick Share contract

- Per-device offer starts off and is independent of shared import defaults. Enabling after import makes eligible history visible; disabling hides the action. Import never starts export. Each action explicitly chooses 1080p/4K and LUT on/off; BT.709 tags cannot identify I-Log.
- Persist only the last 20 completed batches: exact verified original paths/SHA-256, device/profile, destination volume/identity and date. History survives source eject/restart. It never restores connection-bound eject authority and never rescans earlier destination folders for candidate clips.
- Restrict current conversion to `VID_*.mp4` with embedded Luna Ultra/Pro identity, preserving the Mac tail-identity rule. Reject changed originals/destination and HDR/Dolby Vision. Neither FFmpeg/FFprobe nor the manufacturer LUT is bundled. The user supplies the official `Luna_I-Log_to_Rec709_BT1886_s33_v2.cube`; validate the baseline official SHA-256 `e3e5c3ab4ca7c166f3ebb740b45ed8cbce649b0c18dca9d5661754c53b9ee17d`.
- Preserve landscape/portrait framing with even dimensions, no upscale/crop/stretch; cap 30 fps; H.264 at 8 Mb/s (1080p) or 24 Mb/s (4K), stereo AAC 160 kb/s, SDR BT.709 output. Replace VideoToolbox with an explicitly validated Windows encoder/fallback while preserving the recipe's behavior.
- Probe dimensions/duration/audio/color, compare duration within 0.25 s and fully decode video/audio before publication. Recheck originals, flush output, exclusively publish, and write recipe/source/LUT/export hashes. Hash-verified receipt reuse and safe conflict names preserve previous exports. Cancel/timeout cleans only current private work and retains completed copies. Failures retain retry history and report originals unchanged; success returns to a fresh eject choice when invoked there, never ejects automatically.

Use **Public Trust** for public Windows downloads; Private Trust and Public Trust Test are not publicly trusted. A trusted-provider certificate used with SignTool is another supported path. No signing identity/certificate/credentials were supplied or used here, so signing remains pending. [Microsoft trust models](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/artifact-signing/concept-trust-models).

Artifact Signing needs an Azure subscription, tenant, identity validation and certificate profile. Individual public signing currently requires a US/Canada location and matching individual billing/identity information. Ryan's eligibility/accounts have not been verified. Review this after local proof; no accounts or paid services are created here. [Microsoft setup requirements](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/artifact-signing/quickstart).
